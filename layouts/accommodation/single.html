Before you choose

Three small steps

  1. 1

    Register first. This picker is for people who selected and paid for a single or shared sleeping place.

  2. 2

    Find a room. Search a public display name or browse by building and floor.

  3. 3

    Claim one place. Tally gives the final confirmation. A click here does not hold it.

Coming with someone? Places on a double bed or sofa are claimed separately, one registered person at a time. Choose two available places on the same piece of furniture if you want to share it.
