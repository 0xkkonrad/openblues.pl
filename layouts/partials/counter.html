Confirmed! {{ $paid }} people have paid — Open Blues {{ $year }} is happening.

{{- else }}

{{ $paid }} of {{ $threshold }} people have signed up and paid.

Open Blues {{ $year }} happens if {{ $threshold }} people sign up and pay the €50 by {{ site.Params.goNoGoHuman }}. If we do not get there, you get your €50 back.
