Open Blues is a private, cost-shared gathering. Nobody is paid, nobody profits, and there is no ticket: everyone covers their own share of the real costs — the palace, the bands, communal food and their own sleeping place.

So there are only three things to decide, and this page adds them up.

{{< cost-calculator >}}

## The price list

Everyone eats the same communal food and dances in the same ballroom. The only thing you are choosing is where you sleep. Sunday night costs €25 in a tent or on the floor and €50 in a bed.

| Thursday, Friday & Saturday nights | Your cost |
|---|---:|
| Tent or floor | €70 |
| A place in a double bed, per person | €110 |
| A single bed | €160 |

| Sunday night, if you stay | Your cost |
|---|---:|
| Tent or floor | €25 |
| Any bed | €50 |

A donation of €100, €50 or €20 is welcome on top, and entirely optional. It is what keeps the cheapest sleeping place cheap.

Everything except the €50 Reservation Payment is brought in cash to the venue. If you bring Polish złoty instead of euros, use **€1 = 4.50 PLN**.

## What happens to the €50

The €50 Reservation Payment is part of your total and is transferred when you sign up. If fewer than {{< param threshold >}} people commit by {{< param goNoGoHuman >}}, it is refunded; otherwise it is non-refundable. If you cannot come, you may pass your place to someone else by emailing [openbluespoland@gmail.com](mailto:openbluespoland@gmail.com).
