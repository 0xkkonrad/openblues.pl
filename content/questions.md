## Is there a selection?

Everyone who signs up and pays is in. There is no selection, no application, and nothing to wait for.

## When do signups close?

Signups close on {{< param closeHuman >}}.

## Who runs the workshops?

Participants do. The programme takes shape late, so we do not publish a line-up. Say at signup that you might lead something. You can change that answer later.

## Can I bring children or pets?

Yes, as long as they do not bother the farm animals. There are geese, chickens, turkeys (RIP 🙏), dogs and cats. And people.

## I have a dust or animal allergy. Can I come?

It is a farm, so there are animals and some dusty corners. The ballroom and the upstairs rooms are usually clear of both.

## Can I arrive early or leave late?

Yes. Email [openbluespoland@gmail.com](mailto:openbluespoland@gmail.com) before you book your travel.

## How do I get there, and where do I sleep?

[Plan your stay](/booklet/) has the address, the airports, the ride-sharing group and the sleeping options.
