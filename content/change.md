You can change your details yourself, any time — accommodation, Sunday night, donation, whether you'll DJ, jam or run a workshop, your travel and lift offer, and the spelling of your name. The latest answer counts.

## Use the link in your confirmation email

When you signed up we emailed you a confirmation. It carries a link that reopens **your own signup**, with your answers already in it. Change what you like, submit again, and the new answers replace the old ones.

There is nothing else to fill in, no second form, and no version to keep track of. Search your inbox for **Open Blues** and it is in there.

## What you can change

- Where you sleep on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights — a tent or the floor, a place in a double bed, or a single bed
- Whether you are staying Sunday night
- Your optional donation
- Whether you'll DJ, jam or run a workshop
- Your travel: the city you are coming from, and whether you can pick people up from Nysa
- The spelling of your name

Use it as often as you like. Changing where you sleep, Sunday night or your donation changes the cash amount you bring to the venue — work the new number out on the [cost calculator](/cost/).

## What is not self-service

The €50 Reservation Payment, your email address and passing your place to someone else are handled by email: [openbluespoland@gmail.com](mailto:openbluespoland@gmail.com).

There is one case in which the €50 comes back, and changing your answers is not it. The Reservation Payment is not refunded for any other reason. If you cannot come, you may pass your place to someone else — email [openbluespoland@gmail.com](mailto:openbluespoland@gmail.com).

## Your exact sleeping place

If you paid for a bed, you choose your exact place in the shared Sheet, in signup order. Changing your accommodation here does not move your row in that Sheet — [browse the rooms and floor plans](/accommodation/), where the Sheet is linked, and move yourself there.
