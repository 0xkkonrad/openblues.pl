Nobody is paid to fill this. There is no marketing budget and no ads, and the people who cook the
food and teach the workshops are the same people who have to find each other first — so that
number only moves because somebody told somebody.

Which makes the useful thing here something other than a share button. It is one person, in one
city, deciding to bring their scene along. The people you bring are the ones you will be sharing a
car with, cooking with and still dancing with at four in the morning: this is not publicity for a
festival, it is choosing who is in the room.
